# INQUORIGIBLE

## Overview
In the course of volunteer OSINT work I performed on a missing persons (MP) case in May 2025, I became interested (and horrified / disgusted / etc.) by the ... quorrors on that hellsite. I was fortunate enough to be able to present my findings at DEFCON 34 in Recon Village. I had a lot of productive conversations there that left me feeling hopeful (me? hopeful?!) that the OSINT community could positively address these issues. And like any good Internet wormhole, the more I torture myself by poking into it, the more I uncover. So anyway,  I'm documenting my findings and scripts here as part of the ongoing project, providing proof of concept, etc.
